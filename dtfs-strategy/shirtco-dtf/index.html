What still belongs on Shirt.co

Useful production facts moved over. The cheap consumer storefront did not.

  • DTF is CMYK + white on PET film, powdered, cured, then pressed at about 300–320°F for 10–15 seconds.
  • Works on cotton, poly, blends, and dark garments. No pretreatment.
  • Gang sheets pack several designs on one 30" film for shops that cut their own.
  • White-label / drop-ship for other printers. Same building as Shirt.co screen and embroidery.
  • Artwork: transparent PNG preferred for raster; vector AI/PDF when you have it.

FAQ

Moved from DTFs.co, rewritten for Shirt.co

Do I need to order transfers, or will you print the shirts?

If you need wearable goods, we print the shirts. Transfers-only is for shops with a press and print-ready files.

Is there a minimum?

Finished apparel: no hard minimum on most DTF garment jobs. Transfer-only wholesale is not a $3 cart — plan on production that is worth setting up, or use the garment quote.

What file should I send?

Transparent PNG at 300 dpi at final size, or vector. JPEG photos from a phone almost always fail the size check above.

How fast can you turn it?

Garments: 5 business days standard, rush as fast as 24 hours. Transfer-only: 2–3 days, next-day rush when the queue allows.

Can you ship under our shop’s name?

Yes. White-label / blind ship is a sales conversation. Call (636) 926-2777.

Union printing?

Allied Printing Trades Council, Local 76. Ask for the bug when the end customer requires it.
